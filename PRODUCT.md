# Catfolio

## Register

product

## Users

Catfolio is for self-directed personal investors who review their portfolio on a desktop and want a private, trustworthy workspace for holdings, returns, risk, valuation, and strategy research. They need dense financial information to remain easy to scan and should not need professional trading-terminal experience to understand the product.

## Product Purpose

Catfolio is a local-first investment command centre that combines broker holdings, market data, portfolio accounting, quantitative analysis, strategy experiments, and AI-assisted explanation. It should help a user understand what changed, where risk and return come from, and what deserves attention next while keeping private data on the user's machine.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and self-possessed. Catfolio should feel like a thoughtful macOS-native research tool: serious about data, transparent about uncertainty, and warm enough to remain approachable during frequent use.

## Anti-references

- Dark trading terminals with neon market colours.
- Finance branding built from navy and gold.
- Generic SaaS dashboards made from repeated identical metric cards.
- Decorative AI gradients, glassmorphism, and chat-toy styling.
- Marketing-style hero sections inside analytical workflows.
- Interfaces that hide data provenance or present model estimates as account truth.

## Design Principles

1. Treat navigation as the environment and the active workspace as the primary surface.
2. Organise analysis around investor questions, not around chart types.
3. Make data source, freshness, and estimation status visible at the point of use.
4. Keep dense information breathable through alignment, rhythm, and progressive disclosure.
5. Prefer familiar native controls and stable workflows over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access for all primary workflows. Do not communicate gains, losses, freshness, or risk with colour alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain visible focus states, use semantic labels for icons and tables, and support both Chinese and English without clipping or layout breakage.
