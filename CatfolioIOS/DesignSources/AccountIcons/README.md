The fee icon is the oval artwork from [node 657:26818](https://www.figma.com/design/EtNSXmasS8lz9JzrWUoMd9/Catfolio?node-id=657-26818).
The complete 24 × 24 node was exported as SVG with absolute bounds to preserve
its rotation and padding. It is used for the annual fee summary, as specified
by the user. Portfolio tab artwork retains its original selected and unselected
assets.

The original SVG exports retain their 24 × 24 artboards, paths and transforms.
Settings is exported from the whole icon node, preserving its skew and inset
instead of stretching the smaller inner vector group. Asset catalog template
rendering supplies the existing adaptive foreground color in both appearances.
