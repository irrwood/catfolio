Local ledger

Getting started

Do I need a computer or server?

No. The app runs independently. Connect a supported broker or import a CSV on your phone.

Which devices are supported?

Requires iOS 18 or later. Some on-device AI features require iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence-compatible device.
