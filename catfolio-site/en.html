Security & control

Your data. Your permissions.Every decision stays with you.

Your ledger stays on your device and portfolio calculations run locally. Configure your own connections and manage your own data.

01

Calculated on your device.

Holdings, costs, returns, and ledger analysis run on iPhone. CSV files are parsed locally; portfolio calculations do not require uploading your personal ledger to Catfolio servers.

02

Managed by you.

Choose which accounts to connect, records to import, and data to view. Your ledger stays on your device, with trade exports and a local portfolio reset available.

03

Read-only connections.

Broker connections read accounts, holdings, and history. Catfolio has no order placement, cancellation, or transfer features. Strategy Composer is for research and simulation.

04

Access you authorize.

Create dedicated read-only credentials or grant the required read access. API keys and connection credentials are stored in the system Keychain; manage authorization with the relevant provider.

05

Your setup. Independent operation.

Configure supported brokers, market data, and AI services on your phone. Everyday use requires no always-on server or computer.

06

AI you choose.

Apple's local model runs on supported devices. With remote services such as Codex or DeepSeek, relevant questions and analysis context are sent to the selected service.

Know what connects: broker sync, quotes, and FX updates contact their respective data providers. Remote AI processes context through your selected service. Some preferences can sync through iCloud; personal ledgers and broker credentials are excluded from preference sync.

A few things you may wonder.

Can I trade with Catfolio?

Catfolio reads the required account, holding, and historical data. It does not place or cancel orders or transfer funds. Use dedicated read-only credentials or access grants. Make trades in your broker's app.

Do I need a computer or server?

No. The iOS app runs independently. Connect supported brokers on your phone or import CSV transactions through Files.

Where is my data stored?

Your holdings ledger is stored locally on iPhone and credentials in the system Keychain. Quotes, broker sync, and optional AI features contact their respective third-party services.

Which iPhones are supported? Where can I download it?

The app requires iOS 18 or later. App Store and TestFlight links are not yet configured; they will appear here when available.
